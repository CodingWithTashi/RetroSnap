What RetroSnap asks for

iOS asks you before RetroSnap uses any of these. You can change your answer at any time in the iPhone’s Settings app, under RetroSnap.

Needed to shoot

Camera

Shows the live preview and takes your photos and videos. Every look is applied on your phone.

Video only

Microphone

Records the sound for your videos. It is not used for photos. If you say no, videos are recorded silent.

Add only

Photos

Saves a copy of each shot to your photo library. RetroSnap can add photos, but it cannot see the ones already there.

Off by default

Location

Only if you turn on “Location on photos”. RetroSnap then notes roughly where each shot was taken and writes it into that photo.

Where your photos live

Every photo and video you take is saved in RetroSnap’s own storage on your iPhone. With Save to Photos on, a copy also goes to your photo library.

Nothing is sent to us or to anyone else. The app has no server to send it to, and it works the same with no internet connection.

RetroSnap also keeps a few preferences on your phone: the camera you used last, your flash setting and the switches in Settings. It checks how much free space your phone has, so it can warn you before storage runs out.

Location on photos

Location is off until you turn it on in the app’s settings, and you can turn it off again there at any time.

While it is on, RetroSnap reads your location only when the camera is on screen, and asks iOS for an approximate position, accurate to about 100 metres. The place is written into the details of the photo or video you take, in the app and in the copy saved to your photo library, the same way the iPhone’s own camera does it.

It is never stored anywhere else, and never sent to us.

What RetroSnap does not do

  • No analytics or usage tracking inside the app.
  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers.
  • No third-party SDKs.
  • No sign-in, profile or contact details.
  • No selling or sharing of your data. There is none to sell.

What iOS and other apps handle

A few things happen outside RetroSnap, under your own iPhone settings.

Backups and iCloud

If you back up your iPhone to iCloud or a computer, RetroSnap’s photos are part of that backup, like any app’s data. If you use iCloud Photos, the copies in your photo library sync the way all your photos do. Both are covered by Apple’s privacy policy.

Sharing and printing

When you share or print a photo, iOS passes it to the app or printer you choose. A photo taken with location on carries that location with it. What happens next is up to the app you shared it to.

Crash reports

If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers, Apple may send us anonymous crash and usage reports. They never include your photos and they do not identify you. You can turn this off in Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements.

Deleting your data

Delete a photo in RetroSnap and it is removed from the app’s storage once the Undo message has gone. Copies saved to your photo library stay there until you delete them in Photos.

Deleting RetroSnap removes everything the app stored on your phone.

Your rights

Privacy laws such as the GDPR and the CCPA let you see, correct or delete the data a company holds about you. We hold none. Everything RetroSnap makes is on your device and under your control.

Children

RetroSnap does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.
