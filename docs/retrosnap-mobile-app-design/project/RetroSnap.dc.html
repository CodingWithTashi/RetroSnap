RETROSNAP · VINTAGE CAMERA · iOS
A camera, not a photo editor.
The viewfinder is the home screen, the shutter is the biggest thing on it, and the selected look is live before you shoot. Every photo saves itself.
Try in the prototype
Continue → allow → shoot. Tap a camera card or swipe the viewfinder to switch looks. The first photo opens the result; after that, shots fly into the gallery button so you keep shooting. Gallery → photo → Edit, Print, Collage, Delete (with Undo).
Recommendations
Camera selection: bottom strip over the live preview (1a). It keeps the shutter and the look in view.
Controls: five, no more. Flash, Photo/Video and timer at the top. Camera chip, gallery, shutter and flip under the thumb.
Assumptions
Default camera: 35mm Film (the most flattering look). Sample photos are stock stand-ins with the looks applied live in CSS. Swap in real sample shots before review. Restore Purchases is left out of Settings because nothing is for sale.
After capture: one decision
The brief wants a result screen and also a thumbnail that flies to the gallery. I show the result after the first photo only, to teach where photos go, then stay in the camera. This is a Tweak if you want “always” or “never”.

1Camera selection · two layouts
1aBottom strip over live preview Recommended
One tap on a card switches the look and the strip folds back into the chip. The viewfinder and shutter stay visible the whole time, so you choose by seeing the real scene change.
1bFull-screen card carousel
Bigger samples and room for a description, which helps discovery. The cost: it hides the viewfinder and adds a screen between you and the shutter. Good as a “browse all cameras” page later.
